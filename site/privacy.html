Privacy without ambiguity.

Core processing

Selected text, page title, page URL, instructions, and generated results are sent only to the SelectPilot bridge on your computer. The bridge uses your local Ollama runtime. There is no cloud-model fallback in this flow.

Local storage

Settings, runtime state, model consent, and signed access information are stored on your device. Deep memory is opt-in and provides inspect, export, and delete controls.

Payments

Paddle processes checkout information under its own privacy terms. SelectPilot receives the minimum payment state needed to issue and verify access. Selected text and generated output are never sent to Paddle.

Data use

Core content is not sold, used for advertising, or used for credit decisions. SelectPilot includes no product analytics or telemetry in the core runtime.

Your control

You decide when selected text is processed and when a result is exported. You can remove local data by deleting retained history and uninstalling SelectPilot.
